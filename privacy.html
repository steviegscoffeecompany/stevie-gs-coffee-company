Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Stevie G's Coffee Company respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website, contact us, or place an online order.

Information We May Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Billing and payment information
  • Order and pickup information
  • Messages submitted through our website

Our website may also collect basic technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, and website usage data.

Payment Information

Online payments may be processed through a third-party payment processor. Stevie G's Coffee Company does not intentionally store full credit or debit card numbers on this website.

How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Communicate with you about an order
  • Respond to questions or requests
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Comply with legal requirements

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies, analytics services, or similar technologies to understand website traffic and improve your experience. Your browser may allow you to limit or disable cookies.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate our website, process payments, fulfill orders, or comply with the law.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
