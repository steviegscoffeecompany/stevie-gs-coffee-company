Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Online & Pickup Orders

If you need to cancel or change an order, please contact Stevie G's Coffee Company as soon as possible.

Once an order has been prepared or preparation has begun, we may be unable to cancel the order or issue a refund.

Large Orders & Catering

Large orders, catering orders, and specially prepared orders may be subject to separate cancellation terms provided at the time the order is placed.

Store-Caused Cancellations

If Stevie G's Coffee Company is unable to fulfill an order and must cancel it, the customer will receive a refund for the cancelled items.
