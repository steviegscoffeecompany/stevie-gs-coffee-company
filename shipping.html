Pickup & Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Local Pickup

Online food and beverage orders are available for local pickup. Please pick up your order as close as possible to your scheduled pickup time to ensure the best quality.

Shipping

Eligible merchandise, packaged coffee, and other non-perishable products may be available for shipping. Available shipping options, costs, and estimated delivery times will be shown at checkout.

Prepared food and beverages are not available for shipping.

Delivery Times

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed. Stevie G's Coffee Company is not responsible for carrier delays, weather delays, or other circumstances outside our control.

Questions or Problems

If there is a problem with your pickup or shipped order, please contact us as soon as possible so we can help.
