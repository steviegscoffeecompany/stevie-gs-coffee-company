Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Food & Beverage Orders

Because our food and beverages are freshly prepared, they are generally not returnable or refundable.

If your order is incorrect, missing an item, or there is a problem with the quality of your order, please contact us as soon as possible. We will work with you to make it right.

Refunds

When appropriate, Stevie G's Coffee Company may provide a replacement, store credit, partial refund, or full refund. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis.

Merchandise

Unused merchandise may be eligible for return within 14 days of purchase. Gift cards, opened food products, and customized items are non-returnable.
